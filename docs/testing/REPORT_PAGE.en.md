# Reporting a problem

*German original (handed to testers): [REPORT_PAGE.de.md](REPORT_PAGE.de.md).*

Send the filled-in questionnaire and your problem reports **directly and only to the facilitator**, the way they told you (for example by e-mail). No GitHub account needed.

- Reports are **not published**: not in the project, not in the public bug list, not in a shared folder, and not read by any AI tool.
- The facilitator describes the problem in their own words when passing it on; your name and texts are not included.
- If your report has **real data** (question 8: yes), it stays only with the facilitator and is **deleted after the round** (D-552).

One sheet per problem: title; card number; what I wanted; what I expected; what happened (every message word for word); does it happen again (always, sometimes, once); version number (from "About KassenSturz"); computer; screenshot (only if it shows nothing but example data); log (from the developer-mode banner, optional); how bad (blocks me, annoying, minor); contains real data (no / yes). Feel free to say how it **felt**.

**For the facilitator:** reports with real data go to no agent, no project file, no issue and no prompt. Reports without real data are rewritten in the team's words before a bug is filed; no tester wording or screenshots enter the project.
