# Trying KassenSturz: the tester guide

*For testers. Team copy of [TESTER_GUIDE.de.md](TESTER_GUIDE.de.md); the German files are the ones handed to testers (testers are German-speaking, D-553).*

Thank you for trying KassenSturz, a free household-money program for your computer. It is meant to be simple enough that you need no accounting or economics background.

## What this is about

**You are not here to find bugs for us.** We want to learn **how KassenSturz feels to you**: what you see first, what you expect, what you like and what you don't, and how you get along with it. The facilitator guides you through some features.

Not every feature is meant for everyone. Some people only want to see where their money goes, others like to calculate themselves, others build things. So at one point in the session you **choose your own path**.

## What happens

| When | What | About |
|---|---|---|
| Before, at home | Read this guide, install KassenSturz, send the install step log | 20 min |
| Session | Talk with the facilitator: a short practice, some tasks, then a few questions | 30 to 60 min |

The **facilitator** is the person who invited you, sitting next to you or on a video call. They give you **task cards** one at a time. You solve them with KassenSturz and **think aloud**.

## 1. You can't do anything wrong

- **It is about the program, not you.** If you can't find or understand something, that says something about the program.
- **There are no right or wrong answers.** We are interested in your impression as it is.
- **Be open**, even when you don't like something. "I don't understand this" or "this seems awkward" helps more than politeness.
- **"This is not for me" is a good answer.** You may drop any task and go to the next.
- **You may pause or stop at any time**, without giving a reason.

## 2. Thinking aloud

Say **everything that goes through your head** while you use the program, as if telling someone on the phone who can't see your screen. Say what you expect before you click and what happens, and how you feel (unsure, surprised, relieved, annoyed). If you go quiet, the facilitator only asks "What are you thinking right now?" They do not help or explain during the tasks, because nobody sits next to you at home either; your questions are answered at the end. The facilitator practises this with you first.

## 3. Recording and your data

- **Screen and audio are recorded, only if you agree.** The facilitator asks at the start. No is fine; the session runs the same and the facilitator takes notes. You can have the recording stopped at any time.
- **What happens to it:** only the facilitator sees and hears it. It is not published or passed on and is **deleted after 4 weeks at the latest**. Your name is not in anything written from it; you get a short code.
- **How it is recorded:** you work on your own computer and share your screen in a browser video call; nothing to install. It is recorded only at the facilitator's end; no recording stays on your computer.
- **The recording shows your whole screen.** Close other programs, e-mail and chats before the session and mute notifications.
- **Please no real data.** You work in an **example book** with an invented family. Don't type your own accounts, amounts, bank names or IBANs, because everything you enter is on the recording. If the program asks for a balance during installation, invent one.
- **If you do enter something real:** say so at the end. We then treat recording and notes as personal data: they stay with the facilitator and are deleted after the evaluation.
- **Bank and AI stay off.** The session needs no bank and, after installation, no internet. Leave bank connections and AI off, also at home.
- **Only if you choose the path "Ask questions" in the session:** you switch on an AI yourself that the facilitator provides. Your questions and parts of the example book go to the facilitator's computer, not to an outside company. The program asks for your consent first. The conversation is written down. After the session you switch the AI off and your access is blocked.
- **Developer mode.** For the test you start KassenSturz in *developer mode*. The program then writes down which pages and buttons were used and when, with error codes, but **without amounts, names or texts from the book**. "Developer mode" at the top of the window means it runs. It is for tests, not everyday use. If the AI is on, the conversation with it is also written down.
- **Your book is not encrypted.** KassenSturz stores it unencrypted on your computer; anyone who can use your computer can open it. This also holds later when you use KassenSturz for your own money.

## 4. What you need

A computer with **Windows 11 (or 10)** or **Ubuntu**; an internet connection for the download (afterwards not needed); on Ubuntu your computer password once; 30 to 60 quiet minutes for the session.

## 5. Before the session: install alone

**You install at home, alone, with the install sheet only.** Nobody helps or watches, because that first impression is exactly what interests us.

- [Install sheet Windows](INSTALL_WINDOWS.en.md)
- [Install sheet Ubuntu](INSTALL_UBUNTU.en.md)

Write every step into the [install step log](INSTALL_LOG.en.md). If you get stuck, write down where, stop there and send the log to the facilitator; you then get one hint by e-mail. That is valuable information too.

In short: on **Windows** you install from the **Microsoft Store** like any other program there. On **Ubuntu** you install with one command from the Snap Store.

You don't need to read the [handbook](../user/en/README.md) beforehand.

## 6. The session, step by step

1. The facilitator greets you and asks whether they may record.
2. You practise thinking aloud briefly and answer three short questions.
3. You look at the start page of the example book and say what you see.
4. You get some task cards from everyday life, **one at a time**.
5. You choose one of three or four ways to get numbers and charts and try it.
6. At the end a short talk about your impression; then the facilitator answers your questions.

If something occurs to you afterwards, write it on the [report page](REPORT_PAGE.en.md) and send it **only to the facilitator**.
