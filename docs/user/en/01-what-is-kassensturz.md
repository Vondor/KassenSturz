# 1. What KassenSturz is, in two minutes

*This page says what KassenSturz is good for and what it does for you. You don't need any finance words.*

KassenSturz is a **household book** for your computer. In it you keep track of where your money is (in your checking account, in your savings account, in your wallet) and where it goes. From that, KassenSturz shows you at a glance **where you stand**: how much you have, what came in and went out this month, and what for.

## The most important points

- **The overview is the start page.** As soon as KassenSturz opens, you see where you stand. Everything else is one step further, if you want more.
- **Your data stays with you.** The household book is kept on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere without your clear yes. There are no ads and nothing is passed on.
- **The numbers are right to the cent.** KassenSturz calculates with exact amounts and rounds only what it shows, never the calculation.
- **Everything can be taken back.** **Undo** takes back the last change, even after a restart.
- **It never sends money.** KassenSturz reads balances and transactions; it never transfers anything.
- **The AI features are off** until you switch them on yourself. This handbook describes KassenSturz without them; everything here works without AI.

## The few words you should know

| In the app | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Book** (household book) | everything KassenSturz knows about your money, in one file on your computer |
| **Account** | a place where your money is: checking account, savings account, cash, credit card |
| **Transaction** | one single payment or deposit |
| **Category** | what money was spent on or where it came from, for example *Groceries* or *Salary* |
| **Transfer** | money you move between your own accounts; it counts neither as money in nor as money out |
| **Rule** | an instruction to KassenSturz: "Sort everything from this name into this category" |

A longer list is at the end of the handbook (page 20).
