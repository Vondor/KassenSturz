# 13. Budgets

*A budget sets an amount for a spending category, for example for groceries. The overview then shows how much of it is left. It changes no transaction.*

## Setting a budget

1. Open **Budgets** in the menu on the left and choose **Add a budget**. Or choose **Set a budget…** in a category's menu.
2. Under **Category**, choose one or more spending categories. **Count more categories with it** adds more. A category for money in doesn't work; KassenSturz says so.
3. Under **Only count money paid from some accounts** you can choose accounts. Without a tick, money from all accounts counts. That way, a shared household account can have its own budget, apart from your own.
4. Under how often, choose **Every month**, **Every quarter**, **Every year**, **Every salary month** or **Once, for some days** (then with a first and a last day).
5. The **Amount** is what you want to spend.
6. **Carry over what is left** decides what happens at the end of a period: what is left, or what was spent beyond it, moves into the next one. Without a tick, every period starts afresh.
7. **Set the budget** saves it. You may give it a **name** such as "Holiday"; without a name the budget is called after its categories.

## Changing, stopping, deleting

- **Change** takes a new amount from a period of your choice. **Earlier periods keep what they had**, so their results never change.
- **Stop** ends the budget after the current period. The earlier results are kept.
- **Delete** removes the budget with its earlier results. Your transactions and categories stay as they are, and **Undo** brings the budget back.
- A budget's categories and accounts can't be changed yet after it is made. If you want that, stop the budget and make a new one; the old one keeps its past.

## On the overview

As soon as there is a budget, **budget bars** appear on the overview. Each shows how much of the amount is already spent and what is left, and says in words when something was carried over from the period before. In the item's **Properties** you choose which budgets it shows (the default: all).

**If something looks different:** a message such as "None of your budgets covers these days." means the chosen period lies outside all budgets.
