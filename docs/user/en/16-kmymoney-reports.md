# 16. Reports from KMyMoney

*If you come from KMyMoney, you can take your saved reports with you. This page shows how.*

## When you first read in a file

If you start a book from a KMyMoney file (page 3), its saved reports come along as **Saved views** in the Statistic Builder. The last page lists each report under:

- **Kept as they were**,
- **Kept with changes** (with what changed, in plain words),
- **Not kept** (with the reason).

A period such as "Last 30 days" becomes the days it covered on the day you read in the file; they are shown next to it.

## Adding them later

**Add reports from a KMyMoney file…** (Statistic Builder, Saved views) brings the reports of a KMyMoney file into the open book, as often as you like, for example while you still use KMyMoney alongside. Nothing else comes in from the file. Accounts and categories are found by their names; anything your book doesn't have is left out and named. If a view loses its whole filter, KassenSturz says that it now shows more than in KMyMoney.

**Before anything is written**, a page shows each report:

- **New in this file** (ticked; untick what you don't want),
- **Different from the view you have** (your view stays, unless you tick "Replace … with KMyMoney's version"),
- **Already here**,
- **Not kept**,
- views whose report is **No longer in the file** (they stay).

**Add reports** writes what is ticked; one **Undo** takes it all back. Nothing is ever deleted. The file itself is only read.

**If something looks different:** if a report has no counterpart in KassenSturz, it is listed under "Not kept" with the reason; that is not an error message.
