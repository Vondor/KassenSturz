# 18. Settings

*Here you set how KassenSturz looks and speaks. None of it changes your saved amounts.*

## Language

**Language** chooses German or English. KassenSturz then restarts once; nothing is lost.

## How amounts look

**How amounts look** starts with your regional settings: decimal point or comma, thousands separator, where the currency symbol goes. You can change these, and also the number of **Decimal places** (100 or 100.00). This is **only what is shown**: it never changes saved amounts or calculations. Totals are calculated from the exact amounts before they are rounded for display. A rounded amount shows the exact one when you point at it with the mouse.

## Chart colors

**Chart colors** is one palette for all charts (page 14).

## Light and dark

KassenSturz follows your system's light or dark appearance.

## Bank connections, password storage, reminder

See page 11: **Bank connections**, **Password storage** and **Reminder to get new transactions**. The reminder belongs to this computer, not to the book.

## History and backups

**Show history** and **Clean up history…**; **Backups** (page 9).

## Your data

**Save a copy of my data…**, **Open another book…**, **Start a new book…** (pages 3 and 17).

## AI features

Under **AI features** there is one single switch. It is **off**, and as long as it is off, none of it runs and none of it appears in KassenSturz. This handbook describes KassenSturz without AI; everything here works without it.

**If something looks different:** if you can't find a setting, use **Show advanced settings**; some things are one step further away, so the first page stays simple.
