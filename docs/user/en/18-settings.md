# 18. Settings

*Here you set how KassenSturz looks and speaks, and you look after your book. None of it changes your saved amounts.*

**Settings** in the menu on the left starts with the button **Save a backup now…** (page 17). Below it are six groups, the switch for the AI features and the help pages.

## My book

Which book is open and where its file is, **Tags**, and the buttons **Open another book…**, **Try an example book…**, **Start a new book…** and **Start a book from a file…** (pages 3 and 17).

## Bank

**Bank connections** and the **Reminder to get new transactions** (page 11). The reminder belongs to this computer, not to the book.

## Look and language

- **Language:** **As in your system**, English, German (Deutsch) or Polish (Polski). Close KassenSturz and open it again to see it in the new language; nothing is lost.
- **Colors of the app:** KassenSturz uses its own colors, light or dark as your system is set. A theme plugin can give it other colors (see Plugins below).
- **Layout:** how much fits on the screen. A layout plugin can make lists tighter.
- **How amounts look** starts with your regional settings: decimal point or comma, thousands separator, where the currency symbol goes. You can change these, and also the number of **Decimal places** (100 or 100.00). This is **only what is shown**: it never changes saved amounts or calculations. Totals are calculated from the exact amounts before they are rounded for display. A rounded amount shows the exact one when you point at it with the mouse.
- **Chart colors** is one palette for all charts (page 14).

## Backups and undo

**Backups** shows where KassenSturz keeps the copies it makes of your book; **Restore from backup…** brings one back, and **Also keep copies in another folder…** puts copies in a second place, for example on another drive. Under **Undo and history** are **Show history** and **Clean up history…** (page 9).

## Plugins

A plugin is a small file that adds something to KassenSturz, for example another kind of bank file or other colors. Here you add a plugin file you downloaded and see what it may do before you allow it. KassenSturz works completely without plugins.

## Advanced

**Where your book is kept**, **Folders KassenSturz may open** (page 17) and **Password storage**, where remembered banking PINs are saved (page 11).

## AI features

Under **AI features** there is one single switch. It is **off**, and as long as it is off, none of it runs and none of it appears in KassenSturz. The one exception is a single question at the end of the guided setup (page 3), which leaves the switch off unless you answer yes. The numbered pages of this handbook describe KassenSturz without AI; everything there works without it. If you would like to try it, [Using an AI with KassenSturz](ai.md) explains every step.

## About

**Help: patterns, formulas and charts** (page 15), **Help: guided setup** (page 3), **Testing KassenSturz** for testers, and the **Version** number you give when you report a problem (page 19).

**If something looks different:** if you can't find a setting, look in **Advanced**; some things are one step further away, so the first page stays simple.
