## How to read this handbook

- **Labels from the app** are in bold, exactly as you see them on the screen.
- Each page starts with one sentence on what it is for and ends with "If something looks different".
- The screenshots come from the made-up example books. No real accounts, names or banks appear anywhere. They show the app in German; the English labels are in the text next to them.
- Amounts are written the English way, for example €1,234.56. Your app shows them the way your Settings say (page 18).
- KassenSturz gives no tax, legal or investment advice.
