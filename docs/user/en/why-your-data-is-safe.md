# Why your data is safe

*One page for everyone who isn't a computer expert and wants to know whether they can trust KassenSturz with their money. This page is also available [in German](../de/warum-ihre-daten-sicher-sind.md).*

## Your data stays on your computer

- Your household book is a file on **your** computer. There is no online service and no account with us. We never see your data.
- KassenSturz sends no usage data, no crash reports and no ads anywhere.
- KassenSturz connects only when you start it yourself: to your bank, when you fetch new transactions, or to an AI that you switched on and chose yourself.
- From your bank, KassenSturz only **reads** balances and transactions. It can't send money, set up a standing order or a direct debit.

## What the AI sees

- The AI is **off** until you switch it on yourself in Settings. While it is off you see nothing of it, and nothing of it runs in the background.
- You can use an AI on your own computer. Then nothing leaves your computer.
- If the AI is on another computer, even one in your home, KassenSturz first shows **what** would be sent and asks you. Without your agreement nothing is sent.
- The AI only makes suggestions. You see every change first and confirm it yourself; **Undo** takes it back.
- KassenSturz works out every amount and total itself, never the AI.

## When something goes wrong

- **Everything can be undone.** Every change, even a whole imported statement, can be taken back in one step.
- **All or nothing.** If the computer goes off in the middle of a save, afterwards either everything is saved or nothing is. There are no half-saved transactions.
- **Backups:** before a new version adapts your book, KassenSturz makes a backup by itself and keeps the last ten (**Settings › Backups and undo**). A copy of your own from time to time is still wise: [Saving a copy and exporting](17-copy-export.md).

## Open source

- KassenSturz is **free software** (GPL licence). Its whole blueprint, the source code, is public. Anyone can look at what the program does and report mistakes.
- KassenSturz makes no money from your data and shows no ads, now or later.

## What you should do yourself

- **Your book is not encrypted.** It holds no PIN, but it holds your transactions. Whoever gets a copy of the file can read it. So protect your computer with a password, and think of backups and folders that sync with the Internet too.
- Keep your system up to date. KassenSturz can't protect you against malware on your computer.
- Nobody from KassenSturz will ever ask you for your PIN or a TAN.
- Use the stable release for your own household book, and keep a copy of your own from time to time. Beta builds are for trying things out; use an example book or a copy there.
